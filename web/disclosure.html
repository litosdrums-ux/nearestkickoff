Affiliate disclosure

Last updated 23 November 2026

How we are paid

Some matches carry a Find tickets link to Sports Events 365, a ticket seller. Those are affiliate links: if you follow one and buy a ticket, we receive a commission from them. You pay exactly the same price either way — the commission comes out of their margin, not your pocket.

Every one of those links is labelled affiliate where it appears. If a link is not labelled, we earn nothing from it.

What the commission does not buy

It does not decide what the map shows. Every fixture we can source appears, whether or not a ticket link exists for it, and a match with a link is never ranked higher, shown sooner, or given more prominence than one without. We take no payment for placement and no payment to include or exclude a club, a league or a country.

Most fixtures on the map have no ticket link at all — either because Sports Events 365 does not cover that league, or because we have chosen not to link it. Those matches are shown exactly the same way as the ones that earn us something.

Where the link actually goes

Sports Events 365 identifies matches by internal number rather than by name, so we cannot link you straight to a specific fixture. The link opens their page for that competition, which lists its matches and prices. That is why it says "find tickets" and not "buy tickets" — we are sending you somewhere to look, and we cannot promise the match you wanted is on sale.

Why some matches never carry a link

There are no ticket links on English or Welsh fixtures. Section 166 of the Criminal Justice and Public Order Act 1994 makes it an offence for an unauthorised person to offer tickets for a designated football match, which covers virtually all professional football in England and Wales. We are not authorised, so we do not link them. Scottish fixtures are unaffected.

The ticket sale is not ours

We sell no tickets and take no booking. The sale is between you and Sports Events 365, on their terms. Price, delivery, refunds and entry to the ground are theirs to answer for — see our terms. We never see your payment details.

Tips

There is also a Buy me a coffee link in the footer. It is a tip jar, not a paywall: nothing here is behind it and nothing is withheld without it. Payment is handled entirely by Buy Me a Coffee.

No advertising

The site carries no advertising, no banners and no sponsored placements, and it never has.

Why this page exists

Because "how does this make money?" deserves a straight answer rather than something buried in the terms. If how we are paid changes, this page changes first.
