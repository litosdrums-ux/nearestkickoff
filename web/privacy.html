Nearest Kickoff

Privacy

Last updated 23 November 2026

The short version

Nearest Kickoff does not ask who you are, does not set cookies, and does not run analytics or advertising. There are no accounts and nothing to sign up for. When you open the map, your browser downloads the fixture and map data and everything after that happens on your own device.

What we collect

Nothing you type. The town you pick in "where are you" is used by your browser to work out how far each match is. It is never sent to us or to anyone else, and it is not remembered between visits.

No cookies, no local storage. The site sets no cookies and stores nothing in your browser. There is no consent banner because there is nothing to consent to.

No analytics, no tracking, no advertising. There are no measurement scripts, tag managers, pixels or fingerprinting of any kind.

No third-party requests. Everything the page loads — the map library, the fonts, the fixture data, the town list — is served from this site itself. Opening Nearest Kickoff does not tell any other company that you did.

Server logs

The site is static and is served by Cloudflare Pages, which like any web host keeps standard request logs. Those typically record the IP address, the time, the page requested and the browser's user-agent string, and are used to serve the site and guard against abuse. We do not combine them with anything else or use them to build a profile of you. Their retention is governed by the host's own privacy policy.

When you click a link to another site

A match marked at stadium level offers a Directions link, and one marked at town level offers a Search link. Both open Google Maps. We do not load anything from Google unless you click, but once you do, you are on Google's site and their privacy policy applies. Links to source and licence pages behave the same way.

Some matches carry a Find tickets link to Sports Events 365. That is an ordinary link too — nothing from their site loads here, and they are not told you visited unless you click it. If you do, you are on their site under their privacy policy. It is an affiliate link, which means they can tell that the visit came from us; see the affiliate disclosure.

The same goes for the Buy me a coffee link in the footer. It is an ordinary link, not an embedded button: nothing from Buy Me a Coffee loads on this site, so visiting the map tells them nothing. If you follow it and choose to tip, that happens on their site under their privacy policy and their payment processing — we never see your card details, and we are not told who you are unless you tell them to pass on a name.

Your rights

Under the GDPR you have rights of access, correction, erasure, restriction, portability and objection in respect of personal data about you. We hold no personal data about visitors beyond the server logs described above, so in most cases there is nothing to give you — but if you think we hold something about you, write to contact@nearestkickoff.com and we will tell you what there is. You also have the right to complain to your national data protection authority.

Controller

The controller for the limited processing described here is Carlos M, contactable at contact@nearestkickoff.com.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top changes with it. If the change is substantive — new processing, a new third party — it will be noted on the map page too, not buried here.

This page is a plain description of how the site works, written for people rather than lawyers. It is not legal advice, and if the project starts handling payments, accounts or personal data it should be reviewed by someone qualified.
